A first story

01 jan 2026
Your name

Every journal begins with a first story. This is a small space to pause, notice the world around you and share what you find.

A place to begin

I used to wait until I had something big to write about. A finished project, a long trip or an idea polished enough to explain. That left a lot of ordinary days unrecorded.

This time, I am starting with the small things: a good walk, a paragraph from a book and the first rough sketch of a project. They count too.

Keeping it simple

The plan is to write a little, often. Some posts will be stories; others will be a photograph or a short list. Over time, I hope they become a useful record of what caught my attention.
