A familiar street feels different after dark. The traffic keeps moving, but
lit windows and reflections turn an ordinary walk into something worth slowing
down for.

## Looking up

At Plaza de Cibeles, I stopped to look at the towers instead of the crossing
signal. The building glowed against the sky, and for a moment the city seemed
to have a completely different rhythm.

## A small habit

I want to take more walks without a destination. Just one neighborhood, enough
time to look up and no pressure to turn every outing into a plan.

The best part is coming home with something I would otherwise have missed.
