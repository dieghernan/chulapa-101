A little background

Hello

I keep this journal to make a little more room for curiosity. Most days, that means taking the long way home, trying something new or writing down an idea before it disappears.

What you will find here

  • Walks: streets, buildings and small details worth noticing.
  • Projects: experiments that start with a sketch or a few spare minutes.
  • Notes: books, useful links and things I want to remember.

There is no grand plan. A short story and a photograph are often enough. Browse the archive or explore the tags to find a place to begin.
