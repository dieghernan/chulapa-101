Metadata and video examples

Page options in Chulapa 2.1.0

The visible heading, browser title and social title can be configured independently. This page also declares its language and article type. The video thumbnail represents its content in social previews and article structured data.

Video metadata

This example uses the video metadata provided in the Chulapa documentation. Replace all metadata together when embedding your own video. Upload dates use ISO 8601 and durations use ISO 8601 durations, such as PT2M52S.
